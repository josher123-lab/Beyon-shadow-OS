# Academy of Jeet Kune Do Fresno

Official website for the premier Jeet Kune Do martial arts academy in Fresno, California.

## About

This website showcases the Academy of Jeet Kune Do Fresno, where students can learn Bruce Lee's revolutionary martial art philosophy and techniques. The academy offers various programs for all skill levels and ages.

## Features

- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Modern UI/UX**: Clean, professional design with martial arts aesthetics
- **Interactive Elements**: Smooth scrolling, animations, and dynamic content
- **Program Information**: Detailed information about all training programs
- **Class Schedules**: Complete weekly schedule with instructor details
- **Contact Forms**: Easy-to-use contact form for inquiries
- **Instructor Profiles**: Information about certified JKD instructors

## Sections

1. **Hero Section**: Eye-catching introduction with call-to-action
2. **About**: Information about Jeet Kune Do philosophy and the academy
3. **Instructors**: Profiles of certified JKD instructors
4. **Programs**: Detailed program offerings for different skill levels
5. **Schedule**: Weekly class schedule with times and instructors
6. **Contact**: Contact information, location, and inquiry form

## Programs Offered

- Adult JKD Fundamentals (Beginner level)
- Advanced JKD Combat (Advanced level)
- Youth JKD Program (Ages 8-17)
- Women's Self-Defense (All levels)
- Private Lessons (By appointment)

## Bruce Lee's JKD Philosophy

*"Absorb what is useful, discard what is not, add what is uniquely your own."*

The Academy follows Bruce Lee's core principles of Jeet Kune Do:
- Practicality and efficiency in technique
- Adaptation to any situation
- Personal expression in martial arts
- Continuous learning and improvement
