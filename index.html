About Jeet Kune Do

The Art of Fighting Without Fighting

Bruce Lee's Philosophy

Jeet Kune Do, "The Way of the Intercepting Fist," is Bruce Lee's martial art and philosophy. It emphasizes practicality, efficiency, and directness, adapting to any situation with fluid, interceptive techniques.

Our Academy's Mission

At Academy of Jeet Kune Do Fresno, we preserve and teach authentic JKD principles while helping students develop physical fitness, mental discipline, and practical self-defense skills. Our training combines traditional techniques with modern applications.

Core Principles:

  • Absorb what is useful
  • Discard what is not
  • Add what is uniquely your own
  • Express yourself honestly
